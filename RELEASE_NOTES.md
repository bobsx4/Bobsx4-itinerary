# Bobsx4 Road Companion v0.3.0 RC2 — Release Notes

RC2 addresses the first major RC1 usability finding: **Navigator and Explorer felt like the same mode with slightly different wording.** They are now separate experience packs with different content, layout order, tone, visual treatment, journal prompts, observation lists, missions, facts, photo briefs, badges, and tomorrow teasers for all ten travel days.

## Navigator

Designed for an independent teen traveller and co-pilot.

- Navy/indigo field-report visual language.
- `PLAN · OBSERVE · REPORT` mode identity.
- Navigator briefing and co-pilot assignments.
- Route intelligence and decision prompts.
- Field Log observations such as timing, signs, route changes, queues, road engineering, and operational details.
- Deliberate photography briefs.
- Field-note journal prompts about decisions, route changes, purchases, and future-trip advice.
- Navigator-specific credentials and next-dispatch teasers.

## Explorer

Designed for a visual, direct, adventure-oriented traveller.

- Teal/orange visual language.
- `SPOT · TRY · COLLECT` mode identity.
- Today's Adventure briefing and straightforward missions.
- Road Quest appears near the top of the mobile page.
- Concrete I-Spy items: animals, signs, vehicles, scenery, rides, shops, ferries, and landmarks.
- Short facts and visual questions.
- Quick journal prompts for favourite moments, food, purchases, surprises, and memories.
- Explorer-specific badges and more playful tomorrow teasers without using a childish visual style.

## Shared improvements

- The active mode is now unmistakable in the briefing card, profile chooser, header accent, section titles, card shapes, and Memories language.
- Mobile section order changes by mode:
  - Navigator: assignments → route intelligence → photo brief → field log → field notes.
  - Explorer: missions → Road Quest → photo challenge → cool facts → My Day.
- iPad keeps the two-column layout while applying the distinct content and styling.
- Mode-specific sightings and missions now drive badge eligibility and statistics; hidden items from the other mode are not counted.
- The Memories page displays the active profile's badge names, observations, and journal language.
- Existing v3 local state, hotel confirmations, notes, and profile names remain compatible.

## Still deferred

Automatic route-distance recalculation, live family synchronization, photo storage, weather/fuel feeds, and multi-Adventure selection remain deferred. See `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`.
