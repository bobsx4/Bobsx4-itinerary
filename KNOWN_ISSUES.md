# Known Issues and Deferred Capabilities

## RC3.3 test focus

1. Profile data, answers, hotel confirmations, and journals remain local to each device.
2. Photo missions store completion, not the actual photo.
3. Hotel addresses update map endpoints, but displayed kilometres and drive times do not yet recalculate.
4. Weather, traffic, border waits, ferry queues, road closures, and fuel prices remain linked rather than embedded.
5. RC3.3 does not call an undocumented GasBuddy interface.
6. The data model supports multiple Adventures, but the selector/builder is deferred.
7. Switching an established profile between Navigator and Explorer can make the scrapbook less coherent.
8. After a GitHub Pages push, the installed app may show the previous build until deployment completes and Refresh is tapped.
9. Clearing browser site data can remove local progress; export a backup first.
10. Real iPhone and iPad acceptance remains required for keyboard, safe area, orientation, and offline relaunch behaviour.
